Strength Foundation

Advanced

Build raw power and muscle mass with compound lifts. This 8‑week program focuses on bench press, barbell squat and deadlift with linear progression. Maximum calorie burn in minimum time. 20‑30 minute sessions combining sprints, burpees, kettlebell swings, and plyometrics.
